About the service

Your Testing Requirement. Our Coordination.

Not every customer knows which test is required, which laboratory is suitable, what kind of sample is needed, or what information should be shared to move the testing forward. We help you understand your testing requirement and coordinate the next steps with appropriate third-party testing facilities.

Whether you are unsure about the testing method, sample handling needs or reporting process, our role is to support clear communication and help guide the requirement toward a suitable testing solution.
